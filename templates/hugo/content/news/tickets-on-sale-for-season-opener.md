Be there when it matters.

Single-game tickets for the 2026 home opener at Arizona Stadium go on sale
Monday at 10 a.m. MST. Season-ticket holders and Bear Down Fund members get
first crack in a 48-hour priority window that opens Thursday.

Highlights:

- **Home opener** — Saturday, September 5, 6:30 p.m. MST.
- **Family Four-Pack** — four general-admission tickets, four meal vouchers, one price.
- **Student rush** — $5 with a CatCard, gates open 90 minutes before kickoff.

One desert. One season. Bear down.
