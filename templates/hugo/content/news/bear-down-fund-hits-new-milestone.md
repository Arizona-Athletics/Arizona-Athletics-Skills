Twenty-thousand donors strong.

The Bear Down Fund crossed its 2026 spring giving goal three weeks ahead of
schedule, powered by a wave of first-time supporters across Tucson, Phoenix, and
every alumni chapter from Los Angeles to Denver. Every gift — every single one —
funds scholarships, academic support, and the day-to-day work that lets a
student-athlete put on Block A.

This is what 140+ years of Wildcat resolve looks like.

> "Bear Down is a promise we make to the next class," the AD said.
> "Today, twenty-thousand of you made it with us."
