Bear down. Three games, three wins, one ovation that rolled out of McKale and
into the desert air.

Arizona swept the weekend series after a second-half surge sealed it — 24-9 over
the final twelve minutes, with the bench scoring nine of the final fifteen. The
Cats now sit two games clear in the standings with four to play.

> "We didn't blink," the head coach said afterward. "Bear Down is the deadline.
> We just got there first."

Up next: the Cats hit the road for a midweek tilt before returning to Tucson on
Saturday. Single-game tickets go on sale Monday at 10 a.m. MST.
